# Livro de Colorir da Família – Prompts

13 de out. de 2026 · @Alexsandro

## Como usar

Cada cena = ESTILO BASE + personagens que aparecem + descrição da cena. Os blocos abaixo ficam sempre iguais; só a descrição da cena muda. Os prompts estão em inglês porque os geradores de imagem respondem melhor assim.

1. Gere primeiro as folhas de personagem (próxima seção) anexando a foto da família como referência.
2. Escolha a melhor folha de cada personagem e use-a como imagem de referência em todas as cenas.
3. Monte o prompt de cada cena colando os blocos fixos antes do texto da cena.
4. Formato sugerido: quadrado (1:1) ou retrato A4 (3:4), fundo branco.

### ESTILO BASE

```text
Cute kawaii coloring book page, black and white line art only, thick clean uniform black outlines, rounded chibi proportions (big heads, small bodies), simple expressive faces, NO shading, NO gray, NO gradients, NO textures, NO hatching, pure white background, large closed shapes easy to color with a paint bucket tool, few big background elements, simple and uncluttered composition.
```

### PAPAI

```text
DAD: sturdy adult man with broad shoulders, short dark hair faded on the sides with volume combed up and back on top, very full thick beard covering cheeks and chin with mustache, thick eyebrows, big wide toothy smile, friendly eyes, plain short-sleeve t-shirt, large sporty smartwatch on his left wrist.
```

### MAMÃE

```text
MOM: gentle adult woman, long straight hair past her shoulders with a side part, oval face, soft closed-mouth smile, light eyes, small stud earrings, long-sleeve V-neck blouse with a tiny polka-dot pattern.
```

### JOAQUIM

```text
JOAQUIM: little boy about 3 years old, very round face and chubby cheeks, short straight hair with a fringe falling on his forehead, big curious eyes, clever little expression, open button-up denim shirt over a plain t-shirt.
```

### LUNA

```text
LUNA: small fluffy Shih Tzu dog with long soft fur, short muzzle, big round eyes, long furry floppy ears, curled fluffy tail over her back, a topknot tied with a small bow on her head, outlined patches on ears and around the eyes (bicolor fur: white and brown).
```

### NALA

```text
NALA: tricolor calico cat with large clearly outlined separate patches (for black, brown and white), almond-shaped big eyes, pointy ears, long tail, curious and confident "boss of the house" posture.
```

### NEGATIVO (se o gerador aceitar)

```text
color, shading, gray tones, gradients, realistic, photo, text, watermark, logos, brand names, thin sketchy lines, cluttered background
```
